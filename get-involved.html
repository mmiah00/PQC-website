You're In The Right Place

If You're A Woman+ Interested In Quant Finance, Start Here

If you're a woman+ and interested in pursuing a career in quantitative finance but don't know where to start, you've come to the right place! The PQC community is a home to high-achieving women who provide each other with support and encouragement as they navigate coursework, recruiting, and the early stages of a career in a historically male-dominated field. Whether you're just beginning to explore quant finance or already deep in the recruiting process, we're here to help you get there, together.
