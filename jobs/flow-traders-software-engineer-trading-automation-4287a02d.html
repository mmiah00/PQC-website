Software Engineer (Trading Automation)

Flow Traders · New York · $185,000 · Posted Aug 12, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

Flow Traders is looking for a Software Engineer (Trading Automation) to join our Technology department in our New York office. As a member of Trading Automation team, you will develop applications that support our core business by improving data frameworks, automating workflows, and creating opportunities for greater efficiency and growth. In this role, you will be responsible for developing applications, services, mentoring team members and managing stakeholders. Your actions will directly contribute to the performance of the company. You will also get the opportunity to interact and work closely with the global Trading Automation team and different departments to gain a well-rounded knowledge of start-to-end trading landscape, how they function and integrate within our ecosystem.

Requirements

  • 5+ years of professional working experience
  • Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or related fields required; advanced degree preferred
  • Strong working knowledge of Git, SQL, Python, and Linux
  • Familiarity with any of Airflow, Ansible, Kafka, Docker, Kubernetes, and AWS/Azure/GCP is a plus
  • Experience using LLMs to develop software and integrate solutions with existing systems
  • Excellent communication and writing skills
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment with a strong sense of urgency
  • Strong organizational, analytical, and problem-solving skills
  • A hands-on, entrepreneurial mindset
