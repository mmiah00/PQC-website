Full-Time · Quantitative Finance

Risk Analyst

Belvedere Trading · Chicago, Illinois · Posted Nov 24, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

Partner with trading desks and build a deep understanding of trading strategies, products, modeling, and market dynamics Attend weekly trading meetings and performs portfolio reviews with traders as needed Execute day to day processes including start of day risk reviews, enforcement of the Firm’s risk policy, and margin balancing Monitor a variety of standard and proprietary risk metrics throughout the day Identify gaps in portfolio risk analytics and work with modeling to teams to close Work with technology teams to define appropriate safety settings for electronic trading systems and other operations risks Support trading with risk-aware strategies during market dislocations. Provide real-time feedback to and periodic review of respective trading desks

Requirements

  • Required
  • Ability to work and communicate effectively with all levels of management and trading within the company and promptly resolve issues or recommend improvements in a positive manner
  • Demonstrates initiative and an ability to proactively identify, prevent and mitigate risks
  • Strong attention to detail
  • Working knowledge of derivatives pricing, modeling, statistics, and risk
  • Experience with SPAN, RBH and related margining methodologies
  • A strong desire to automate
  • Proficiency with MS Office
  • Nice to have
  • 3+ years of experience in a risk department in an options market making firm or clearing firm
  • Python or C++ relevant experience
  • Working knowledge of the regulatory requirements associated with SEC Rule 15c3-5
  • Options trading or market making experience is a plus
