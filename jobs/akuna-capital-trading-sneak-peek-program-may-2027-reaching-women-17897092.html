Trading Sneak Peek Program: May 2027 (Reaching Women+)

Akuna Capital · Chicago · Posted Nov 19, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

Requirements

  • Graduating between December 2027 – July 2028
  • Pursuing a BS/MS/ PhD in engineering, economics, statistics, mathematics, computer science, actuarial science or a related/equivalent field
  • Able to attend in person at Akuna’s Chicago headquarters
  • Entrepreneurial self-starter ready to work in a fast paced, collaborative, team environment
  • Strong math, numerical, and quantitative skills
  • Demonstrable interest in trading and the financial markets
  • In addition to technical skillsets, Akuna values the unique perspectives people can bring to the table to collaboratively solve complex problems and drive Akuna forward. We want everyone to feel empowered to apply. We welcome your application and encourage you to take the first steps toward your future with us!
  • Please note: By submitting an application for the May 2027 (Reaching Women+) Trading Sneak Peek Program, you acknowledge you will not be considered for other Trading Sneak Peek Program sessions (January and August 2027) at Akuna this recruiting season. This acknowledgement does not limit applications to other Tech and/or Quant roles - in addition to this program, you may apply to any Tech and/or Quant roles of interest.
  • This is an unpaid educational program. Akuna provides round-trip travel, housing, lunch, and social events for all participants.
