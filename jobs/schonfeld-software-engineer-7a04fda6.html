Full-Time · Quantitative Development

Software Engineer

Schonfeld · New York, New York, United States · $175,000 · Posted Oct 15, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

We are seeking a highly motivated and talented technologist to join us as a member of our Accounting and Valuation Technology (AVT) team. The team is responsible for the ecosystem which provides accurate T0 and T+1 Position, P&L & Transaction Data via its shadow accounting system (Advent Geneva) to key organizational stakeholders in the Fund Accounting, Valuation, Operations, Compliance, Risk and Business Analytics areas. The AVT team directly supports the reporting and automation needs of the Fund Accounting and Tax teams, including internal tools for investor allocations, portfolio manager performance payout estimations and withholding tax calculations. The team is also responsible for providing end-of-day valuations for listed and OTC products traded across the firm.

Requirements

  • Three to six years of experience in cloud-based Java microservices development, preferably in Spring Boot, with a strong grounding in multi-threading, memory optimization, and object-oriented programming and design patterns.
  • Demonstrated ability to build and implement scalable, standards-based web service API
  • Experience interfacing with message broker or streaming platforms (Kafka preferred)
  • Experience working with relational databases and optimizing performance (PostgreSQL preferred)
  • Experience working with large scale data pipeline and task orchestration (e.g. Apache Airflow, Prefect or similar)
  • Experience working in an Agile project environment, including story estimation
  • Experience with code reviews and measuring code quality through the use of static analysis tools such as SonarQube
  • Experience using AI tooling in day-to-day development
  • Familiarity with equity, fixed income, and/or derivative trade lifecycle and valuation.
  • Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal
  • Strong ownership experience and a track record of delivering quality results
