Develop and improve AI-driven quantitative investment strategies in financial markets within the dedicated China research team. Support all strategies from alpha generation through to trade execution and implementation, working closely with other team members to design, build, and enhance strategies using cutting-edge machine learning and AI techniques. Core responsibilities include: Alpha Generation & Idea Discovery: Leverage large language models (LLMs), NLP, and alternative data pipelines to identify novel investment signals and innovative data sources Data Gathering & Refinement: Apply AI-powered data processing and feature engineering techniques to gather, clean, and structure complex datasets for modeling at scale. Modeling & Statistical Analysis: Design and implement deep learning, reinforcement learning, and statistical ML models to build, backtest, and refine various quantitative models.
