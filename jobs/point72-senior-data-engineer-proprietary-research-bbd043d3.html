Full-Time · Quantitative Development

Senior Data Engineer, Proprietary Research

Point72 · Bengaluru, India · Posted Oct 5, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

Senior Data Engineers on the Proprietary Research team design and build Data & AI Platform to transform structured and unstructured data into actionable insights. As a member of our Data Engineering team, you will work closely with investment professionals, researchers, and data scientists to design, build, and launch robust end-to-end business solutions including data pipelines, products and tools that help extract the most value out of Point72’s data assets. In this role, you will:• Design and build big data pipelines for ingestion, transformation, trend creation, and delivery of AI-ready data assets.• Develop reusable software components, libraries, and APIs to support experimentation and production deployment.• Implement data quality, lineage, and governance controls to ensure datasets are reliable, auditable, and compliant.• Build scalable, AI-native, cloud-based infrastructure using distributed systems and modern cloud technologies.• Optimize system performance, cost, and reliability through benchmarking and continuous improvement.• Explore emerging Data and AI technologies, run proof of concepts, and convert successful ideas into business deliverables.• Partner with investment, research, data science, and compliance teams to turn research needs into production-ready data products and analytics.• Provide hands-on technical leadership, influence architecture and team processes, and support development efforts across engineers and stakeholders.• Use AI as a core operating layer by deploying LLM-based tools and building agentic workflows for business impact.• Demonstrate strong project management, business intuition, collaboration, ethical standards, and a solution-oriented mindset in a fast-paced environment.
