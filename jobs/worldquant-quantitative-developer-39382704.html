Quantitative Developer

WorldQuant · Hanoi OR Ho Chi Minh City · Posted Aug 14, 2026

WorldQuant develops and deploys systematic financial strategies across a broad range of asset classes and global markets. We seek to produce high-quality predictive signals (alphas) through our proprietary research platform to employ financial strategies focused on market inefficiencies. Our teams work collaboratively to drive the production of alphas and financial strategies – the foundation of a balanced, global investment platform. WorldQuant is built on a culture that pairs academic sensibility with accountability for results. Employees are encouraged to think openly about problems, balancing intellectualism and practicality. Excellent ideas come from anyone, anywhere. Employees are encouraged to challenge conventional thinking and possess an attitude of continuous improvement. Our goal is to hire the best and the brightest. We value intellectual horsepower first and foremost, and people who demonstrate an outstanding talent. There is no roadmap to future success, so we need people who can help us build it. The Role: We are looking for a Quantitative Developer to join our team in Vietnam. This is an opportunity for a technically strong, early-career candidate to build the automation and tools that support critical business workflows. You will work alongside experienced engineers to solve technical problems, develop robust internal platforms, and improve the efficiency of complex systems. You will also gain hands-on exposure to production environments by helping monitor workflows, investigate issues, and support on-call activities Monitor production systems, scheduled jobs, data processes, and automated workflows; identify and bring up issues appropriately Investigate technical issues across applications, data processes, and infrastructure; contribute well-tested fixes under guidance Design, build, and maintain internal tools, automation, interfaces, and services that improve business workflows Improve system reliability, observability, documentation, and team productivity Collaborate with engineers and stakeholders to understand requirements and translate them into practical technical solutions Contribute to code reviews, testing, release validation, and user documentation Assist the on-call team with incident triage, diagnostics, communication, validation, and follow-up activities Identify repetitive manual tasks and build automation to reduce operational overhead
