Digital Assets Quantitative Researcher

Flow Traders · Amsterdam · Posted Sep 16, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

Flow Traders is looking for a Senior Quantitative Researcher to join our Digital Assets business. This is a unique opportunity to join a leading proprietary trading firm with an entrepreneurial and quantitative culture at the heart of its business. As a firm, we are committed to leveraging advances in computer science, statistics, and machine learning to generate value in the financial markets. As a Senior Quantitative Researcher, you will be responsible for expanding our Digital Assets trading capabilities by leveraging Flow Traders’ cutting-edge research and quantitative trading platform.

Requirements

  • 3+ years of experience in systematic trading.
  • A proven track record of developing profitable trading or systematic market-making strategies.
  • A nuanced understanding of financial markets, electronic trading, and market microstructure.
  • Demonstrated success translating modern quantitative methods into profitable trading strategies.
  • Experience working with large-scale market datasets, machine-learning operations frameworks, and high-performance computing.
  • Excellent communication skills and experience managing stakeholders across Trading and Technology.
  • A strategic vision for the role of machine learning and AI in the future of algorithmic trading.
  • Experience in Digital Assets markets is preferred.
