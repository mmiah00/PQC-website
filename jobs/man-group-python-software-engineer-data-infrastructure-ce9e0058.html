Python Software Engineer - Data Infrastructure

Join our Data Platform Engineering team at the heart of a quantitative investment firm, powering the data infrastructure which drives our systematic trading strategies and cutting-edge discretionary research. As a Python Engineer, you’ll help architect our multi-petabyte scale estate, processing billions of datapoints daily from thousands of data sources. Our stack combines Java and Python, with foundations of Kafka, ArcticDB, Airflow, Spark, and more. This is an opportunity to tackle complex distributed systems challenges at an exceptional scale across thousands of unique datasets. AI agents are fast becoming primary consumers of our data, which makes improving its discoverability and usability for both people and agents a source of green-field work for the team. Build and ship features on a high-throughput platform processing billions of market data points and thousands of files daily Tackle hard distributed systems problems at a scale few Engineers encounter Work closely with quant researchers and traders to turn complex requirements into elegant platform solutions Drive platform reliability through rigorous incident response, root-cause analysis, and systemic fixes Raise the engineering bar through thoughtful code review and mentoring of junior team members
