Internship · Quantitative Research

Quant Researcher Internship

Man Group · London · $253.6 · Posted Sep 4, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

Research, develop and implement trading strategies. Contribute to AHL's overall research effort by collaborating with teams across AHL. Follow industry/academic literature for innovations and suggest how AHL can profit from this. Apply quantitative techniques and market intuition to large, often novel or unconventional, datasets and cultivate areas of expertise along the way. Contribute to the business' overall research effort by interacting with and collaborating with other research teams.

Requirements

  • PhD, Masters or Bachelor’s Degree in a quantitative subject
  • Desire to learn and understand the structure and drivers of returns in financial markets
  • A demonstrable interest in algorithmic trading, financial derivatives, and markets in general
  • Independent research experience
  • Practical coding experience in a scripting language such as Python is preferred, though not essential
  • Aware of what statistical and mathematical tools and techniques are available and able to independently decide what is appropriate to use in which circumstances
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills
  • Entrepreneurial team player, able to push and discuss views with the rest of the team and contribute to our wider research agenda
