Full-Time · Quantitative Trading

Trading - Trader (Market Making) - London

B2C2 · London · Posted Oct 5, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

We are hiring a Market Making Trader to help run and grow our quoting on centralised exchanges. You will manage automated market making strategies, monitor risk and inventory in real time, and work with quants and developers to improve pricing, execution and profitability. This is a high impact role at the cutting edge of digital asset trading. Manage and optimise the strategies used to make markets on crypto exchanges. Responsible for the PL of team and individual strategies. Develop and improve the models and strategies using quantitative methods. Collaborate with Quant Dev and Research during the full lifecycle of alpha and signals development and deployment. Conduct research and data analysis on bespoke trading data.

Requirements

  • University degree in a numerical degree, with strong grades.
  • 3+ years as a market maker or systematic/electronic trader, in crypto or traditional markets.
  • Strong grasp of market microstructure: order books, queue dynamics, adverse selection, latency and fees.
  • Good knowledge of perpetual futures, funding rates, basis and margin mechanics.
  • Solid programming and data skills, ideally Python and SQL, for analysis and tooling.
  • Hands-on experience running automated quoting strategies and tuning their parameters.
  • Entrepreneurial mind set and a natural intuition for risk, pricing, and how crypto markets function.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced fluid environment at the cutting edge of trading and technology within the industry.
  • Excellent teamwork and clear communication skills and ability to express ideas.
  • Calm, quick decision-making under pressure, with sound risk discipline.
  • Willing to work flexible hours as part of a 24/7 coverage model.
  • A strong culture is a common denominator among the most successful companies and B2C2 is proud to be a values-based company.

  • Innovative - Forward looking in our solutions for clients and employees
  • Meritocratic - Rewarding positive contribution across the organisation
  • Collegiate - Achieving through teamwork through a healthy environment
  • Risk Aware – Informed decision making and considered in our practices
