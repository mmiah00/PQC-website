Senior Software Engineer, Native Mobile (iOS/Android)

Coinbase · Remote - USA · $186,065 · Posted Jun 25, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

Ready to do the most impactful work of your career? At Coinbase, we are uncompromising on our mission to increase economic freedom. The bar is high, the environment is intense, and we like it that way. This isn't a place for complacency, it’s a place to be pushed past your perceived limits. If you're ready to build the future of finance alongside people who refuse to settle for "good enough," you belong here. Coinbase is a remote-first, but not remote-only company. Expect to get together quarterly for intense in-person working sessions called “surges.” Learn more about working at Coinbase. We're hiring a native mobile engineer to join Coinbase's Financial Services and Loyalty product group. The team owns our Credit Card, Debit Card, payments, cash, and loyalty products (Coinbase One), along with our onchain products for borrowing and lending. This role sits at the center of a significant transition: the product group is moving from React Native to native Swift and Kotlin in a greenfield app rewrite. That rewrite is built inside an AI-assisted code generation workflow, with the goal of delivering a dramatically faster, higher-quality experience to millions of users. As an engineer on this team, you'll bring deep iOS or Android expertise to the core foundation of the new native app — setting design and development standards and acting as a technical leader for the broader organization.

Requirements

  • 5+ years of iOS or Android software engineering experience in Swift or Kotlin.
  • A track record of building and shipping performant mobile applications, with attention to frame stability, cold start optimization, and responsive UI across a diverse device population.
  • Proficiency in Swift or Kotlin with working knowledge of the other, and genuine willingness to build cross-platform fluency — engineers on this team own both iOS and Android.
  • A strong point of view on design and user experience, with real investment in product polish and interaction detail.
  • Responsible use of generative AI: human oversight on AI-generated output, with measurable gains in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.
