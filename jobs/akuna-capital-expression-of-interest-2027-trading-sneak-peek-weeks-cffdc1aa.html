Expression of Interest: 2027 Trading Sneak Peek Weeks

Akuna Capital · Chicago · $2,250 · Posted Jul 19, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

Register your interest for our 2027 Trading Sneak Peek Weeks! Thank you for your interest in Akuna's Trading internship! We are excited to share that we will be hosting multiple Trading Sneak Peek Weeks throughout 2027, rather than a traditional summer internship. These one-week micro-internships are designed to provide an accelerated, hands-on introduction to the tools, skills, and knowledge our trading teams use to drive success every day. With this new format, we are excited to welcome even more trading-focused interns to our office in 2027! By submitting this application, you’ll be added to our list of interested candidates and will be notified as soon as the roles goes live.

Requirements

  • Graduating between December 2027 – August 2028
  • Pursuing a BS/MS/ PhD in engineering, economics, statistics, mathematics, computer science, actuarial science or a related/equivalent field
  • Able to attend program in person at Akuna’s Chicago headquarters
  • Entrepreneurial self-starter ready to work in a fast paced, collaborative, team environment
  • Strong math, numerical, and quantitative skills
  • Demonstrable interest in trading and the financial markets
  • Legal authorization to work in the U.S. is required on the first day of employment including F-1 students using OPT or STEM
  • In addition to technical skillsets, Akuna values the unique perspectives people can bring to the table to collaboratively solve complex problems and drive Akuna forward. We want everyone to feel empowered to apply. We welcome your application and encourage you to take the first steps toward your future with us!
  • In accordance with Illinois Equal Pay Act, the total pay for this one-week program is $2,250. The total pay herein was determined in good faith by Akuna Capital LLC.
