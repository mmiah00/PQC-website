Full-Time · Quantitative Development

Senior Software Engineer - Post-Trade Platform

Flow Traders · Amsterdam · Posted Oct 28, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

Flow Traders is building the next generation of its post-trade platform, covering trade capture, reconciliation, settlement, booking, reporting, and operational workflows across Equities, ETFs, Fixed Income, and Crypto. We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to take end-to-end ownership of key post-trade domains, helping shape the platform architecture while building reliable, automated, and data-driven services. This role is ideal for an engineer who enjoys solving complex problems, thinking in systems, and building high-quality platforms that deliver real business impact.

Requirements

  • 5+ years of experience building distributed backend systems using Java or C++, with exposure to Python
  • Strong understanding of distributed systems, data structures, algorithms, and event-driven architectures
  • Experience with messaging technologies such as Kafka
  • Hands-on experience building data-intensive systems, ETL pipelines, and analytical data solutions
  • Strong SQL and data modelling skills
  • Proven ability to own complex technical domains from design through production support
  • Strong stakeholder management and communication skills
