Software Engineer - Business Analytics

Schonfeld · New York, New York, United States · Posted May 29, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

We are seeking a highly qualified embedded Software Engineer to join our Business Analytics team. The Business Analytics team at Schonfeld focuses on analytics, reporting and automation. As a Software Engineer you will work on building key infrastructure to help compute performance related fees for our PMs and other related projects.

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or related quantitative field.
  • 2-5 years of relevant work experience.
  • Strong programming skills in Python and SQL.
  • Interest or experience in capital markets or finance.
  • Strong analytical thinking, attention to detail, and clear communication skills.
  • Experience with Linux operating systems, Git, and cloud computing platforms.
