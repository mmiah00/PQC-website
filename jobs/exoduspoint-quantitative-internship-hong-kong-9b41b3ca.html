Quantitative Internship – Hong Kong

ExodusPoint · Hong Kong · Posted Nov 13, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

The Systematic Rates team researches and runs fully systematic, data-driven strategies in global interest FICC markets. We combine solid statistics, careful engineering and a good feel for how markets work to build and improve systematic trading strategies. You'll work closely with the portfolio manager. You'll do hands-on research and development across the whole strategy process, and you'll own real projects with real impact, not side tasks. Research, prototype and backtest systematic strategies across FICC products such as swaps and foreign exchange. Build and maintain production pipelines, research tools and analytics libraries. Run statistical analysis on market data: signal research, factor analysis, regime analysis, and transaction cost and capacity studies. Help monitor and analyse the performance and risk of live strategies. Write clean, tested, well-documented code that the team can rely on and reuse.

Requirements

  • Strong coding skills. You're proficient in Python (or a similar language). You can design, write, debug and explain your own code. We expect real engineering ability, not just prompting AI tools for code you can't reason about.
  • Strong math and statistics. You're comfortable with probability, linear algebra, regression and time-series analysis.
  • Basic knowledge of financial instruments. You understand FICC market at a foundational level, and you're keen to learn more.
