Quantitative Analyst, PCAT

Point72 · Hong Kong · Posted Nov 17, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

A Career with Point72's Office of the CIO Team The Office of the Chief Investment Officer, led by co-CIO Harry Schwefel, supports the continued development of our Fundamental Equities investment professionals, portfolio construction, and liquidity groups, and continues to advance the tools and resources available to our teams. As a Quantitative Analyst on the Portfolio Construction and Analytics Team (PCAT) in the Office of the CIO, you will use data, analytics, and models of investor behavior and the market to improve investment decision-making. Your work will support portfolio managers and senior management in understanding performance, risk, and capital allocation. Specifically, you will: Analyze the firm’s fundamental equities portfolios to identify strengths and weaknesses in idea generation, trading, and portfolio construction Develop top-down views on strategy risk and return to help senior management allocate capital across investment teams Create new analytics to quantify investment skill and support trade-offs between sources of skill and risk Conduct independent research on investor behavior, market conditions, and performance drivers to identify drivers of success Quantify market drivers to support pre-trade risk taking and decision-making Build analytical frameworks that support trade-offs between different sources of skill and risk Communicate research findings clearly to portfolio managers, the team, and senior investment leadership Develop and contribute to shared code base, reports, and research tools Work with investment and analytics colleagues to refine questions, test hypotheses, and deliver practical insights
