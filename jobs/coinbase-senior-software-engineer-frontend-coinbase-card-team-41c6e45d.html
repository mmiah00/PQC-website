Frontend Software Engineer - Coinbase Card Team As a Frontend Software Engineer on the Coinbase Card team within the Consumer Product Group, you'll build and ship the user-facing experiences behind Coinbase's card products, including the Coinbase One Card (credit) and the Coinbase Card (debit). This team is consolidating both products onto a unified card platform, and you'll join a small team with high ownership, greenfield feature work, and direct impact on how millions of customers discover, activate, and use their cards.

Requirements

  • 5+ years of software engineering experience with deep expertise in React and React Native
  • Proven track record of shipping consumer-facing products end-to-end in a high-scale environment, with a focus on performance, reliability, and user experience
  • Experience owning SLOs and driving measurable improvements in latency and availability for user-facing flows
  • Demonstrated ability to mentor junior engineers and operate as a senior technical voice on a team of mixed levels
  • Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality
  • Pay Transparency Notice: Base salary varies by location (see range below). Total compensation may also include equity and bonus eligibility, and benefits (medical, dental, vision, 401(k)).
