Talent Pool: Graduate Quantitative Trader

Flow Traders · New York · Posted Jul 29, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

Flow Traders is building a pipeline of talented, driven, competitive, and intellectually curious individuals who may be considered for future Graduate Trader opportunities in our growing Trading team in New York. Please note that this is a talent pool rather than an active opening. While we are not currently hiring for an immediate Graduate Trader start date, we welcome applications from candidates interested in being considered should a suitable business need arise. Applications will help us connect with potential future talent and may be reviewed as opportunities become available. This is a unique opportunity to begin your career at a proprietary trading firm, where entrepreneurial thinking, innovation, and collaboration are central to how we operate. This program begins with an intensive three-month training program in Amsterdam. During this program, you would build a foundation in Flow Traders’ core business, the financial products we trade, market dynamics, pricing strategies, and proprietary trading systems. Through quantitative coursework, trading simulations, and instruction from Senior Traders and Quantitative Researchers, you would develop the skills needed to make informed, split-second decisions in dynamic financial markets. Following the training program, you would join a trading desk in New York, where you would progressively take on greater responsibility, monitor markets, identify opportunities, and contribute directly to your desk’s performance.

Requirements

  • University degree, preferably in a STEM-related field
  • Demonstrable interest in trading and global financial markets
  • Understanding of financial products
  • Excellent mental math, quantitative, and analytical skills
  • Proficiency in Python
  • Ability to think on your feet, reason logically, and deliver under pressure
  • Entrepreneurial and competitive mindset, with an eagerness to innovate and find creative solutions
  • Desire to thrive in a fast-paced, team-oriented work environment that offers significant challenges and rewards
  • Please note: Submitting an application does not indicate that there is a current vacancy or guarantee consideration for a specific start date. Candidates may be contacted if a relevant Graduate Trader opportunity becomes available.
