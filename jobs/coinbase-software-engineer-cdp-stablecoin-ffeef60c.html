Full-Time · Quantitative Development

Software Engineer, CDP - Stablecoin

Coinbase · Remote - Canada · $146,300 · Posted Aug 6, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

Ready to do the most impactful work of your career? At Coinbase, we are uncompromising on our mission to increase economic freedom. The bar is high, the environment is intense, and we like it that way. This isn't a place for complacency, it’s a place to be pushed past your perceived limits. If you're ready to build the future of finance alongside people who refuse to settle for "good enough," you belong here. Coinbase is a remote-first, but not remote-only company. Expect to get together quarterly for intense in-person working sessions called “surges.” learn more about working at Coinbase. The Stablecoin team builds and operates Coinbase Custom Stablecoins (CCS), the platform that lets partners launch their own branded, USDC-backed stablecoins on Coinbase's infrastructure. As a Software Engineer on this team within the Developer group, you'll build and extend the backend services and onchain contracts that power issuance, reserves, minting and burning, and rewards distribution for a fast-growing roster of partners. Build and extend smart contract functionality for minting, burning, and safeguarding custom stablecoin supply, working closely with senior engineers on design and security review. Implement backend services that power onboarding, issuance, reserve accounting, and rewards distribution. Collaborate with engineers, designers, and product managers to turn the stablecoin roadmap into shipped features every quarter. Develop reporting, monitoring, and internal tooling that improve reliability and operational excellence for the team. Write high-quality, well-tested code across both backend and smart contract surfaces.

Requirements

  • 2+ years of experience building and maintaining production backend services.
  • Exposure to writing, testing, or deploying smart contracts (Solidity or similar EVM languages), or demonstrated ability to ramp quickly in new technical domains with mentorship.
  • Comfort working within a service-oriented architecture and contributing to established systems at scale.
  • Familiarity with Golang and blockchain fundamentals (Ethereum or similar EVM chains).
  • Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.
  • Demonstrates the ability to responsibly use generative AI tools and copilots (e.g., LibreChat, Gemini, Glean) in daily workflows, continuously learn as tools evolve, and apply human‑in‑the‑loop practices to deliver business‑ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in efficiency, cost, and quality.
