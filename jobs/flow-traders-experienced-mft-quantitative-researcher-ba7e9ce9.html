Full-Time · Quantitative Research

Experienced MFT Quantitative Researcher

Flow Traders · Hong Kong · Posted Sep 21, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

Flow Traders is looking for an Experienced Quantitative Researcher who specializes in mid-frequency research to join us in our Hong Kong office. This is a unique opportunity to join a leading proprietary trading firm where quantitative research, technology, and an entrepreneurial culture are at the heart of our business. In this role, you will identify and develop new trading opportunities by leveraging Flow Traders’ advanced research capabilities and quantitative trading platform.

Requirements

  • A master’s degree in a STEM discipline, computer science, or a related quantitative field; a PhD is preferred
  • 3+ years of experience in quantitative trading
  • A proven track record of profitable mid-frequency trading strategies, operating on intraday to few-day horizons, in APAC equities and/or futures markets
  • Strong grounding in time-series modeling, signal research, and portfolio construction across short holding periods
  • Understanding of market microstructure and the construction of mid-frequency signals from order book and tick data
  • Demonstrated success translating modern quantitative methods into profitable trading strategies
  • Excellent communication skills and experience managing stakeholders across trading and technology
  • A strategic vision for the role of machine learning and AI in the future of systematic trading
  • Flow Traders does not accept unsolicited resumes from any professional staffing or search firms. All resumes, and any other information identifying potential candidates, submitted to any employee at Flow Traders via-email, the Internet or directly without a valid and signed search agreement will be deemed free to contact by Flow Traders without any restrictions and no placement fee of any kind will be paid in the event the candidate is hired by Flow Traders.
