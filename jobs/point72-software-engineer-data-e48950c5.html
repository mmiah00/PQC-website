Software Engineer - Data

Point72 · New York, NY · $200,000-$300,000 · Posted Jun 22, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

A Career with Point72's Technology Team As Point72 reimagines the future of investing, our Technology team is constantly evolving our firm’s IT infrastructure and engineering capabilities, positioning us at the forefront of a rapidly evolving technology landscape. We’re a team of experts who experiment and work to discover new ways to harness open-source solutions, modern cloud architectures, and sophisticated Artificial Intelligence (AI) solutions, while embracing enterprise agile methodologies. Our commitment to building and innovating in the AI space provides the framework intended to drive smarter decision making and enhance how we build and operate our platforms and applications. As a member of Point72’s Technology team, we encourage and support your professional development from day one—helping you advance your technical skills, contribute innovative ideas, and satisfy your own intellectual curiosity—all while delivering real business impact for our multi-billion-dollar global business. As a software engineer, you will help build the next generation of data infrastructure at the firm—thinking holistically about systems across APIs, data models, cloud infrastructure, and user experience. As demand for direct data access, enriched datasets, and AI-powered workflows grows, you will help create a unified, enterprise-grade data platform with clear ownership, shared standards, and the organizational structure to deliver trusted data at scale. Specifically, you will: Build and maintain agentic workflows and tooling that leverage large language models Define the service contracts, data models, and integration patterns that will serve as future firmwide standards for data access Build high-impact, enterprise-grade data services and pipelines that support research, trading, and AI projects across the firm Ensure backend systems are reliable, observable, and performant at scale Containerize services and orchestrate workloads to ensure resilient, observable, and scalable deployments together with cloud engineers Contribute to CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure as code, and deployment automation
