Internship · Quantitative Trading

Quantitative Trading Intern - Winter Quarter 2027

Belvedere Trading · Chicago, Illinois · Posted Aug 4, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

Requirements

  • Communication – We need people who can speak confidently and concisely, especially when the stakes are high
  • Analytical – We’re looking for quantitative thinkers and master problem-solvers with strong data analysis skills
  • Team Player – Trading and development are not isolated activities at Belvedere, so great candidates will have demonstrated success in working in team-based environments
  • Curious – We'll teach you everything you need to know, but we love working with people who ask questions and are eager to get involved
  • Core development skills – You will need some training or a demonstrated ability to learn programming languages such as C++, C#, and Python
  • AI Fluency – Effectively use AI tools to improve efficiency and effectiveness while maintaining independent judgement
  • Knowledge of the financial markets and trading industry is a plus but not a prerequisite
  • Regular attendance is an essential function of this position
  • Ability to live in Chicago and attend the internship fully in person during the winter quarter of 2027 (January - March)
  • Graduation date of December 2027/Spring 2028
