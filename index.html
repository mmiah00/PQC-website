Our Mission

Building A Community For Women In Quant Finance

Phoenix Quant Collective (PQC) builds a focused, professional community at UChicago to support and advance women in quantitative finance. We've seen the gender gap in this industry first-hand, and we wanted to create a space where other women+ feel more seen, supported, and equipped to pursue careers in quant finance with confidence. Through career development, resource sharing, and structured engagement with alumni and industry partners, we're building a community that strengthens recruiting outcomes while fostering genuine support for current and future students.

Who

Undergraduate and graduate students who are passionate about quantitative finance and committed to supporting and advancing women in the field.

What

Career development events, alumni panels, office visits to Chicago-based quant firms, and a centralized hub for recruiting resources and opportunities.

Why

To build a stronger community for women in quantitative finance by creating opportunities for mentorship, professional development, and meaningful connections with peers and industry professionals.
