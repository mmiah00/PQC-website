Our Mission

Building A Community For Women In Quant Finance

Phoenix Quant Collective (PQC) builds a focused, professional community at UChicago to support and advance women in quantitative finance. We serve as a platform for career development, resource sharing, and structured engagement with alumni and industry partners — strengthening recruiting outcomes while fostering a supportive environment for current and future students.

Who

Undergraduate and graduate students in UChicago's Financial Mathematics (FinMath) program and beyond who are passionate about advancing women in quantitative finance.

What

Career development events, alumni panels, office visits to Chicago-based quant firms, and a centralized hub for recruiting resources and opportunities.

Where

Based at the University of Chicago, with programming on campus and on-site visits to partner firms across Chicago.
