Our History

How We Started

Phoenix Quant Collective (PQC) was founded in Spring 2026 to build a focused, professional community supporting women in quantitative finance at UChicago. The idea grew out of conversations among Financial Mathematics (FinMath) students about strengthening recruiting outcomes, career development, and mentorship — and creating a structured way to engage with alumni and industry partners.

PQC's approach is inspired by the success of similar initiatives at peer institutions, including the Columbia Lioness Quant Club and CMU Women in Quant, both of which have built meaningful relationships with firms and alumni to create targeted opportunities for their students.

How We're Getting Off The Ground

Spring 2026

Gauge Interest & Setup

Held a general interest meeting with FinMath women and other interested students, formed a founding board, and canvassed campus with flyers to build interest across undergraduate and graduate programs. By late May, the response was strong enough to launch our Instagram, LinkedIn, and Slack alongside a mailing list.

Summer 2026

Prepare Initial Programming

Building out initial programming and beginning outreach to alumni and industry partners ahead of the fall launch.

Fall 2026

Official Launch

Launching Phoenix Quant Collective with at least one to two flagship events for the semester.

What We Do

Our Focus Areas

A small number of high-impact activities designed to strengthen recruiting outcomes and build lasting industry relationships.

Firm Office Visits

Office visits to Chicago-based quantitative finance firms, giving members a first-hand look at the industry.

Alumni Engagement

Alumni panels and coffee chats, virtual or in-person, connecting students with industry professionals.

Career Development

Recruiting-focused events and ongoing sharing of opportunities, applications, and resources through our Slack.

Participation is open in accordance with university policies. PQC is focused on supporting and advancing women in quantitative finance, and all members, events, and communications are expected to uphold a high standard of professionalism and respect.

Office visits to Chicago-based quantitative finance firms, alumni panels, coffee chats, and recruiting-focused career development events — targeting one to three events per quarter.

Opportunities, applications, and resources are shared on an ongoing basis through our Slack, mailing list, and social media (LinkedIn and Instagram). See our Opportunities page for current postings.
