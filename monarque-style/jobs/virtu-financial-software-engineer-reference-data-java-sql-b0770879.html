Full-Time · Quantitative Development

Software Engineer - Reference Data (Java/SQL)

Virtu Financial · New York · $175,000 - $200,000 · Posted Mar 18, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

We are looking for a seasoned engineer to build and maintain the system that produces essential reference data that is critical for most systems throughout Virtu: trading systems, quantitative research systems, and back office systems. We offer a dynamic, fast-paced, meritocratic environment with a flat hierarchy. Our teams are passionate about continually improving and raising the bar; learning and teaching are keys to Virtu’s culture. Aggregate, validate, enrich, and distribute big datasets from many big data vendors (Bloomberg, LSEG) Build, test, implement, and maintain various databases and API Architect custom software solutions for business stakeholders to improve daily management and evaluation of data across the firm Support large-scale migrations for global, multi-asset data Monitor day-to-day operation of the production system Perform proactive and reactive system maintenance

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field
  • At least 5 years of enterprise-level, distributed systems experience in Java
  • Strong understanding of relational databases (SQL, Oracle RDBMS)
  • Interest in trading and financial markets
