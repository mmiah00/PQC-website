Full-Time · Quantitative Development

Staff Infrastructure Engineer

Flow Traders · Amsterdam · Posted Aug 6, 2026

← Back to Opportunities

Flow Traders is looking for an experienced Staff Infrastructure Engineer in our Amsterdam office. The ideal candidate is proactive and driven, with a genuine appetite for modernizing and continuously improving our technology estate. This is a unique opportunity to join a leading proprietary trading firm with an entrepreneurial and innovative culture at the heart of its business. We value quick-witted, creative minds and challenge them to make full use of their capacities. As a senior individual contributor within the Enterprise Systems domain, you will define the long-term technical architecture and engineering standards across the estate, including identity, endpoints, compute, virtualization, operating systems, storage, databases, and the platforms beneath our enterprise SaaS. Reporting to the Global Head of IT, you will act as the senior technical authority and a mentor to our engineers, driving automation, engineering best practices, and modernization. This is a hands-on, senior IC role rather than a people-management position: an opportunity to shape technical direction and build, not to manage a team.

Requirements

  • 10+ years of senior enterprise infrastructure engineering experience at Staff, Principal, or Technical Lead level
  • Deep expertise in identity and access management, cloud platforms, virtualization, and enterprise infrastructure
  • Proven success leading cloud migrations, legacy platform retirements, and large-scale modernization initiatives
  • Strong architectural judgment with a vendor-neutral approach to cloud and platform decisions, choosing the right target on merit rather than by default
  • Experience integrating enterprise SaaS platforms through SSO, automated provisioning, APIs, and secure connectivity
  • Hands-on experience with automation, Infrastructure as Code, and applying AI to improve operations
  • Strong stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence and drive cross-functional alignment
  • A collaborative technical leader who mentors engineers, raises engineering standards, and thrives as a hands-on individual contributor in a global, multi-region environment
