Senior Software Engineer, Developer Infrastructure

Coinbase · Remote - USA · $186,065 · Posted Jun 8, 2026

Ready to do the most impactful work of your career? At Coinbase, we are uncompromising on our mission to increase economic freedom. The bar is high, the environment is intense, and we like it that way. This isn't a place for complacency, it’s a place to be pushed past your perceived limits. If you're ready to build the future of finance alongside people who refuse to settle for "good enough," you belong here. Coinbase is a remote-first, but not remote-only company. Expect to get together quarterly for intense in-person working sessions called “surges.” learn more about working at Coinbase. Senior Software Engineer, Developer Infrastructure Coinbase’s Developer Infrastructure group builds the systems every Coinbase engineer relies on to write, validate, ship, and run code. We own the path from commit to production, including CI and build infrastructure, deployment platforms, and test systems that give engineers fast, trustworthy feedback. We’re hiring Senior Software Engineers across several teams in the group, and you’ll be matched to the team that best fits your strengths and interests. Wherever you land, you’ll build and operate foundational platform services used across Coinbase engineering, where improvements in build speed, deploy safety, and test reliability scale across thousands of engineers. This is high-leverage systems work with a high bar for reliability and security.

Requirements

  • 5+ years building and operating production software, with strong distributed systems fundamentals and proficiency in Go or a similar language.
  • Track record of shipping high-impact backend or platform systems with ownership from design through production.
  • Strong operational instincts with a focus on reliability, security, and observability.
  • Clear communication and the ability to drive projects independently while collaborating across teams.
  • Customer-focused mindset, with success measured by impact on other engineers.
  • Experience in developer infrastructure or platform domains such as CI/CD, build systems, release tooling, test infrastructure, or developer productivity.
  • Experience operating at scale with Kubernetes, AWS, GitHub Actions, Terraform, and containers.
  • Depth in areas such as build systems, remote execution, progressive delivery, or test optimization.
  • Experience with observability and performance tooling such as Datadog.
  • Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.
